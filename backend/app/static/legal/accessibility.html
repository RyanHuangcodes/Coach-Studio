Our commitment

Coach Studio is committed to making its coaching tools usable by as many people as possible, including coaches with disabilities. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, which are the internationally recognized standard for digital accessibility.

What we have done

  • High-contrast dark theme with large, bold typography for readability in bright outdoor and indoor court conditions.
  • Semantic HTML structure (headings, buttons, labels, and form fields) so screen readers can interpret the interface.
  • Text labels on form controls and aria-label attributes on icon-only buttons.
  • Keyboard-operable controls and dialogs, with focus management on modal windows.
  • Large, well-spaced tap targets intended for one-handed use by a coach in motion.
  • Responsive layouts that reflow for phone, tablet, and desktop screen sizes.

Known limitations

We are continually improving. Some areas are still being brought fully in line with WCAG 2.1 AA, including:

  • Some color combinations and interactive states may not yet meet the minimum contrast ratio in every context.
  • The precision-timer ring and progress indicators convey some information visually; equivalent text is provided but may not be exhaustive.
  • Full compatibility across every screen reader and browser combination has not been independently audited.
This statement reflects a good-faith, self-assessed effort. It has not been verified by an independent third-party accessibility audit.
