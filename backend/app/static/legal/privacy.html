Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

This policy is a template to help you get started. Before relying on it publicly, replace the highlighted placeholders and have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction. It is not legal advice.

1. Who we are

Coach Studio ("we", "us", "the Service") is operated by Ryan Huang, located in Ontario, Canada. For any privacy question, contact us at ryanyuxuanhuang@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Your name and email address.
  • A securely hashed version of your password. We never store your password in readable form and cannot recover it.

Information you enter about your coaching

  • Player names, skill tiers, and free-text notes you add to player profiles.
  • Attendance records, lesson plans, drills, groups, and completed-session history.
  • Any text you type into the AI feature (for example, your training objective for the day).
Important — data about others, including minors. The roster you build may contain personal information about your players, who may be children. You are responsible for having the appropriate authority or consent (for example, from players or their parents/guardians) before entering that information. See section 7.

Technical information

  • A session cookie used to keep you logged in.
  • Standard server logs (such as IP address and request times) used for security and to operate the Service.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the Service: authentication, saving your plans, rosters, attendance, groups, and history.
  • To generate AI drill and strategy suggestions when you request them.
  • To secure the Service, prevent abuse, and enforce rate limits.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Third parties we share data with

We share the minimum necessary data with service providers that help us run Coach Studio:

  • Anthropic — when you use an AI feature, the objective text you enter, along with contextual details such as roster size and skill tiers, is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate suggestions. Review Anthropic's terms and privacy practices at anthropic.com.
  • Our hosting and database provider — [e.g. your host] stores the Service's data on our behalf.

These providers are permitted to use your data only to provide services to us. Where required by law, we put a data-processing agreement in place with each provider that handles personal data on our behalf, governing how they may store, use, and secure it.

5. How we protect your data

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (a one-way function); they are never stored or transmitted in readable form.
  • When the Service is accessed over HTTPS, information in transit is encrypted with TLS.
  • Access to your plans, roster, and history is scoped to your account only.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies

We use a single, essential session cookie to keep you logged in after you sign in. It contains an opaque session identifier, not your password, and it is required for the Service to work. Because it is strictly necessary to provide a service you have requested, it does not require a consent banner under EU/UK rules.

We do not use advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics cookies. If we ever introduce optional analytics or similar non-essential cookies, we will first show visitors in the EU/UK a cookie-consent banner and only set those cookies with your consent.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active, and for up to 2 years after your account becomes inactive, after which we delete or anonymize it. You may also request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting ryanyuxuanhuang@gmail.com, and we will action your request within 30 days, except where we must retain certain records to comply with law.

Requests about a player (including a minor). If you are a player, or a parent or guardian of a player whose information a coach has entered into Coach Studio, you may contact us at ryanyuxuanhuang@gmail.com to ask that we remove that information. Because the coach — not us — decides what player data to enter, we may need to work with the relevant coach to identify and delete it, and we will do so within a reasonable period.

8. Children's privacy

Coach Studio is intended for use by coaches, who must be adults (see the Terms). The Service is not directed to children, and children may not create accounts.

Because a coach's roster may contain information about players who are minors, you agree that you will only enter such information where you are legally permitted to do so and have obtained any consent required by applicable law (for example, verifiable parental consent under COPPA in the United States, or an appropriate lawful basis under GDPR/UK GDPR in Europe). Wherever possible, we encourage you to minimize what you record about minors — for example, using first names or initials rather than full identifying details. If you cannot obtain the consent the law requires, you should not enter a minor's personal information into the Service. If you believe information about a child has been provided to us improperly, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Data breach notification

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no service is completely secure. If we become aware of a personal-data breach that affects your information, we will investigate promptly and, where the law requires it, notify affected users and the appropriate authorities without undue delay — in many jurisdictions within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach. Our notice will describe, as far as we can, what happened, what information was involved, and the steps you can take.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact ryanyuxuanhuang@gmail.com. If you are in the EEA/UK, our lawful basis for processing is performance of our contract with you and our legitimate interest in operating the Service.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, take reasonable steps to notify you.
